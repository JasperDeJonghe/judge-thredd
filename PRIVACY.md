# Privacy Policy – Judge Thredd

**Data we process:** Your Discord user ID, username, and the number of messages you send in tracked channels. We do not store the content of your messages.

**Why:** To maintain a leaderboard of active members and assign a role to the top helper.

**Storage:** Data is stored on the server where the bot runs. The leaderboard is reset after each role award.

**Sharing:** We do not share your data with third parties.
